Repayment
How the Debit Order Works
📝
You Authorise
You sign a debit order mandate as part of your application, giving KatDesign Holdings permission to collect repayment from your bank account.
📅
Due Date Set
Your repayment date is exactly one calendar month after disbursement. You'll receive an SMS reminder 3 days before.
🏦
Auto Deducted
The full repayment (principal + 30%) is automatically collected from your bank on the due date. No action needed from you.
Eligibility
Who Can Apply?
🏛
This loan is exclusively for government-sponsored students currently enrolled at UB, BAC, GIBS, Limkokwing, GUC, or another recognised accredited institution in Botswana.
Requirements
  • Active government sponsorship (DPSM, HRDC, BOTA, etc.)
  • Currently enrolled at a recognised institution
  • Botswana citizen or resident
  • Age 18 or above
  • Valid bank account for debit order
Documents Needed
  • National ID (Omang) or passport
  • Student ID card
  • Government sponsorship letter
  • Proof of enrolment / registration form
  • Bank account details (for debit order setup)
